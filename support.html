Support

A quick guide to capture, editing, text recognition, and purchases.

  1. Start recording

    Open Scrollet and tap Start recording. Confirm the system recording prompt, then switch to your target page during the countdown.

  2. Scroll steadily

    Scroll through the content with overlapping screen areas. Avoid jumping so far that consecutive views have no shared content.

  3. Return to Scrollet

    With Auto finish enabled, capture finishes after scrolling stays still for about one second. You can switch back manually to generate the long screenshot; the system recording indicator and completion prompt may take about five additional seconds to finish.

How do I stop manually?

Tap the red recording indicator at the top of the screen and confirm stopping. Return to Scrollet to generate your image.

Part of the long image is missing. What can I try?

Try scrolling more slowly with more overlap. After returning, adjust the fixed top or bottom areas if needed, then use the manual generate button to stitch again. If there is no reliable overlap, make a new recording.

Can I copy only part of recognized text?

Open text recognition from the long-image result, wait for recognition to finish, then press and hold the text and adjust the selection handles. You can also copy the whole result.

Why are purchase plans or prices unavailable?

Check your network and reload the plans. Prices come from the App Store region used for purchases, not the app’s interface language. Restore purchases with the Apple account used for the original purchase if Pro access is missing.

How do I cancel or restore a subscription?

Use Restore purchases in Scrollet to recheck your purchases. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Settings → your name → Subscriptions. A one-time purchase does not renew. Refund requests are handled by Apple.

Will reinstalling reset the free allowance?

Free usage is stored in this device’s Keychain and can survive a normal reinstall. Reinstalling does not reliably reset the allowance.

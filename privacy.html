Privacy Policy

Your screenshots stay on your device.

Local processing

Scrollet captures screen frames through Apple ReplayKit to produce long screenshots. Image stitching and text recognition with Apple Vision run on your device. We do not send your screenshots or recognized text to a developer-operated server. Microphone audio is not processed or saved by Scrollet.

Temporary images and exported results

Captured frames and session information are temporarily stored in the local shared container used by the app and its recording extensions. Starting a new recording clears the previous capture. Reopening the app normally clears an inactive capture; an active recording or a capture explicitly opened through its completion route is preserved. Images you save to Photos or share are kept in the destination you choose and are not removed by this cleanup.

Free usage and settings

Settings and free usage records stay on your device. The free usage record includes the current quota period, counts, and fingerprints used to avoid charging the same result twice. It is stored in a device-only, non-synchronizing Keychain record and may remain after uninstalling and reinstalling the app. We do not upload this record or use it to track activity across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Scrollet checks verified transaction information on the device to determine access to Pro features. We do not receive your payment card or bank details. Apple handles payment, renewal, refunds, and transaction records under its own terms and privacy policy.

Permissions and sharing

Screen capture begins only after you start recording through the system interface. Access to add images to Photos is requested when you save a result; Scrollet does not request access to read your entire photo library. Copying or sharing text and images happens at your request. Once shared, the destination app or service handles that content under its own policies. Photos exported to your library may also sync through your own iCloud Photos settings.

Analytics and advertising

Scrollet does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or cross-app advertising tracking. Local diagnostic files are not automatically uploaded to us. Data collected independently by Apple services is governed by Apple’s policies.

This website and support

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This site does not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies, or contact forms. Support uses GitHub Issues: submitting feedback requires a GitHub account, and your GitHub username and submitted content are public. The developer uses information you choose to submit to respond to your request. Do not post passwords, payment information, screenshots containing private information, or other sensitive content. GitHub handles your account and site activity under its own privacy statement.

Your choices and policy updates

You can stop recording through the system recording indicator, remove saved images in Photos, manage shared copies in their destination, and manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account. This policy will be updated if Scrollet’s data handling changes; the date above identifies the current version. Contact the developer with questions about your privacy.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the developer at GitHub Issues

Submit feedback on GitHub

GitHub feedback is public and requires a GitHub account. Do not post private information.

Include your iOS version, Scrollet version, and a short description. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or screenshots containing private information.
